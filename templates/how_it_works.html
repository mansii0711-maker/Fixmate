How FixMate Works

A simple, transparent, and secure local service experience for Customers & Providers

For Customers

1

Browse & Search Services

Search for required home services such as electrician repairs, plumbing fixes, house cleaning, appliance maintenance, or home tutors. Filter by location and category.

2

Select Service & Preferred Slot

Review provider profiles, ratings, qualifications, and upfront prices. Pick your date and convenient time slot for technician visit.

3

Service Delivery & Real-time Updates

The service provider reviews and confirms your appointment request. Track your booking status directly on your Customer Dashboard.

4

Pay & Share Review

Once the service is completed satisfactorily, pay cash-on-service or online, and leave a verified rating & feedback review for the technician.

For Service Providers

1

Register & Submit Verification Document

Sign up as a provider, detail your experience and qualifications, operating area, and upload your ID or trade certificate.

2

Admin Review & Profile Approval

Our administration team verifies your credentials. Once approved, your profile and services become visible to local customers.

3

Receive & Manage Bookings

Accept or manage incoming service requests via your dedicated Provider Dashboard. Mark jobs as Confirmed or Completed.
